Welcome to STT Cloud! ...

Here’s what this voice-powered bulletin board can do:

  • Your audio will be transcribed...
  • No usernames, no thread names...
  • Say whatever you want, but...
  • Any language is welcome! Thanks to Whisper...

This is a completely experimental project — just me bringing a small idea to life: What if there was a bulletin board where people could post only by speaking?

Enjoy this little sandbox! The future of this platform is in your hands.

Note: This app is running on a low-spec server...
